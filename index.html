01 — ABOUT PROJECT

Turning Wind Into Electrical Energy

Wind energy is a renewable form of energy obtained from the kinetic energy of moving air. A wind turbine converts this kinetic energy into rotational mechanical energy and subsequently into electrical energy.

Our prototype demonstrates this principle on a small scale using turbine blades, a shaft, a generator and an electrical load or storage system.

Project Objective

To design and demonstrate a small wind-energy conversion system and understand the relationship between wind speed, turbine dimensions and electrical power generation.
